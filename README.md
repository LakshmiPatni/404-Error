# 🛒 Amazong: The Chaos Store

> **Live Demo:** https://amazong-chaos-store-fpkq.bolt.host
---

Welcome, brave soul. You have stumbled upon **Amazong**, an intentionally frustrating, psychologically exhausting online shopping game designed to push your patience to its absolute limit. 

Forget quick and easy shopping. Here, every click is a struggle, prices go up based on your school grades, and the website itself is your worst enemy.

---

## 🚀 Key Features of Psychological Torture

* **A Visual Mess:** Built with blinding neon colors, wacky fonts, and shifting buttons to give your eyes a hard time.
* **Familiar Yet Evil Categories:** Browse through electronics, makeup, fashion, and more, all priced in Indian Rupees (₹) with hilarious product descriptions.
* **The 4-Click Cart Rule:** Trying to add an item to your cart? Good luck—the button will make you click it 4 times while teasing you.
* **Annoying Pop-Ups:** Random popup windows will pop up out of nowhere to ruin your flow and test your calm.
* **The "Intelligence Tax" Checkout:** 
  * You have to enter your academic grades (CGPA) before paying.
  * Answer 4 silly philosophical questions.
  * **The Catch:** The more words you type in your answers, the higher your final bill goes!
* **The Hall of Tortures (Leaderboard):** See how you rank against other resilient shoppers based on your survival time and patience score.
* **Certificate of Survival:** Make it all the way to the end, and you'll get a downloadable, official-looking certificate certifying that you survived maximum psychological torture.
